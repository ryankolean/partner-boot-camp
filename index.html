Chapter 1

How we got here

Before there was a program, there were two people figuring it out as they went. This is Krysta and Ryan's story, so far.

  1. February 5, 2020

    Most compatible

    Hinge flagged them as each other's Most Compatible match, at 96%. The algorithm was onto something. On a bored Sunday, Krysta was the one who sent the first message.

    Ryan was a week into a ski trip in Wisconsin, so the early conversation happened from a long way off. It was enough to make both of them want to meet.

  2. That same week

    The ten-hour drive

    The ski trip ended with a ten-hour drive home, and the first date came right after it. Ryan was running on fumes and nearly asleep.

    He showed up anyway. Looking back, that pretty much set the tone: show up, even when it would be easier not to.

  3. February 2020

    A first kiss in the snow

    The night ended out back of Fourth Street Corner Bar and Grill in Ferndale, with the snow coming down.

    Not the most glamorous spot for a first kiss, and that is part of why it stuck. It earned a second date.

  4. March 2, 2020

    I love you. Twice.

    Ryan's birthday was a bar crawl, and Krysta could not believe how many friends showed up for him.

    Somewhere along the way, about a month after they met, he told her he loved her. Then he said it again.

  5. A week later

    The quiet week

    After the birthday, Krysta went quiet for a week. Then she reached back out, and they picked up where they left off.

    They had met just six weeks before the world shut down. Two people who were still getting to know each other suddenly had to figure things out fast.

  6. About nine months in

    A home of their own

    About nine months in, there was a house, and a month later Krysta moved in.

    Moving in together in the middle of a pandemic is its own crash course in partnership. It turns out they were good at it.

  7. September 2, 2021

    Engaged

    A question, a ring, and a very easy yes.

    Nobody knew yet that the date was going to get used twice.

  8. September 2, 2023

    Married, two years to the day

    Two years to the day after the engagement, they got married. Same date, on purpose.

    Ryan's vows included a promise you will hear about in a minute. It involves an airport and a bouquet.

  9. Now

    Oak Park, paint on everything

    They sold the first house, moved to Oak Park, and are renovating another one to rent, so there is always a paint roller somewhere nearby.

    Somewhere along the way, friends started asking Ryan for advice, and Krysta started telling the stories that led to this.

Chapter 3

What you'll learn together

Four levels that follow a relationship from the first dates to the long haul. Start wherever you are.

  1. Level 1

    The basics

    The dating foundation. Good morning texts, flowers for no reason, picking up the bill, asking how their day really went, and listening to the answer.

  2. Level 2

    Committed partners

    A shared emotional language. Talking about the hard stuff, working through conflict without keeping a tally, and really hearing each other.

  3. Level 3

    Building a home

    Owning your share of the household, keeping the small promises, and making home the best place either of you can be.

  4. Level 4

    The long game

    Keeping desire and connection alive for years to come. Optional modules for expecting a baby, with more paths on the way. Every track is optional, never an either-or.

Chapter 4

Give more. Stop keeping score.

80 / 80

Fifty-fifty sounds fair until you start counting.

We build on the idea at the heart of The 80/80 Marriage by Nate and Kaley Klemp: both of you give more than you expect to get back.

Some days one of you is running on empty. Other days you have extra to give. Nobody keeps score, because you are on the same side.

Chapter 6

Partners cheering on partners

When the doors open, you will not be doing this alone. Partner Boot Camp starts with a live kickoff, then carries on online with a community of couples who swap ideas, share small wins, and root for each other.

  • Effort gets celebrated. Nobody needs to be perfect to belong here.
  • No call-outs, no comparisons. Every couple here is already doing something right.
  • What gets shared stays kind. This is a place to be generous, with each other and with yourselves.
