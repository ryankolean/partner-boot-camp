Chapter 1

How we got here

Before there was a program, there were two people figuring it out as they went. This is Krysta and Ryan's story, so far.

  1. February 5, 2020

    Most compatible

    Hinge called it. Krysta messaged on a bored Sunday while Ryan was a week into a ski trip in Wisconsin.

  2. That same week

    The ten-hour drive

    The first date came right after a ten-hour drive home. Ryan was running on fumes and nearly asleep. He showed up anyway.

  3. February 2020

    A first kiss in the snow

    Behind Fourth Street Corner Bar and Grill in Ferndale, with the snow coming down. It earned a second date.

  4. March 2, 2020

    I love you. Twice.

    His birthday bar crawl. Krysta could not believe how many friends showed up. Ryan told her he loved her, then said it again for good measure.

  5. A week later

    The quiet week

    Krysta went quiet for a week. Then she reached back out. Six weeks after that the world shut down, so they figured things out fast.

  6. About nine months in

    A home of their own

    They bought a house. Krysta moved in a month later.

  7. September 2, 2021

    Engaged

    A question, a ring, and a very easy yes.

  8. September 2, 2023

    Married, two years to the day

    Same date, two years later, on purpose.

  9. Now

    Oak Park, paint on everything

    They sold the first house, moved to Oak Park, and are renovating another one to rent. Somewhere along the way, friends started asking Ryan for advice.

Chapter 3

What you'll learn together

Four levels that follow a relationship from the first dates to the long haul. Start wherever you are.

  1. Level 1

    The basics

    The dating foundation. Good morning texts, flowers for no reason, picking up the bill, asking how their day really went, and listening to the answer.

  2. Level 2

    Committed partners

    A shared emotional language. Talking about the hard stuff, working through conflict without keeping a tally, and really hearing each other.

  3. Level 3

    Building a home

    Owning your share of the household, keeping the small promises, and making home the best place either of you can be.

  4. Level 4

    The long game

    Keeping desire and connection alive for years to come. Optional modules for expecting a baby, with more paths on the way. Every track is optional, never an either-or.

Chapter 4

Give more. Stop keeping score.

80 / 80

Fifty-fifty sounds fair until you start counting.

We build on the idea at the heart of The 80/80 Marriage by Nate and Kaley Klemp: both of you give more than you expect to get back.

Some days one of you is running on empty. Other days you have extra to give. Nobody keeps score, because you are on the same side.

Chapter 6

Partners cheering on partners

When the doors open, you will not be doing this alone. Partner Boot Camp starts with a live kickoff, then carries on online with a community of couples who swap ideas, share small wins, and root for each other.

  • Effort gets celebrated. Nobody needs to be perfect to belong here.
  • No call-outs, no comparisons. Every couple here is already doing something right.
  • What gets shared stays kind. This is a place to be generous, with each other and with yourselves.
